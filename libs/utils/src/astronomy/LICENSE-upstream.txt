The solar position and sun-times formulas in this directory are ported from
SunCalc 2.0.1 (https://github.com/mourner/suncalc). What changed in the port:

- instants are Temporal.Instant, never Date, and the results are typed;
- the sun times are a typed record (rise, set, the twilights, the golden hour,
  noon and nadir) with the observer height as an option, and the Moon's position,
  illumination and rise/set are kept; only the custom-time registry (`addTime`)
  has no counterpart;
- the literal coefficients are named after what they are: the Meeus series are
  coefficient arrays with the formula numbers, the ΔT fits a table of eras, and
  the refraction and rise/set thresholds are derived from their published
  arc-minute and degree values rather than written out as rounded radians;
- the fixed iteration counts of the transit and rise/set solves became a stated
  convergence tolerance of one second with a safety bound on passes;
- every borrowed value cites a public source next to it (NASA, USNO, NOAA).

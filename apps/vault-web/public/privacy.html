Ciphra Vault — Privacy

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Ciphra Vault has no server and no account. It runs in your browser or on your phone and sends nothing to us.

Where your vault is

On your device, encrypted, and — if you choose — as one encrypted file in your own Google Drive or WebDAV folder. Only someone with your master password, recovery key or a passkey you added can open it.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for access to the files it creates (or to its hidden app folder) and uses that access only to read and write your vault file. The token stays in your browser. You can revoke it at any time in your Google account.

Other services the app may reach

  • Have I Been Pwned's password range API — only if you turn on the breach check, and only the first five characters of a password's SHA-1 hash leave the device.
  • 2FA Directory — a public list of sites with two-factor sign-in, for the audit.
  • icon.horse and favicone.com — a site's icon, by its domain, if site icons are on.

No analytics, no crash reports, no third-party scripts.
